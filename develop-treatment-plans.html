Before you start

Who approves the treatment plan?

The treating clinician approves the proposal following clinical assessment. Planning support does not replace this responsibility.

Can I request changes?

Requests are discussed before approval. Their scope and arrangements are defined in your offer.

What if a further opinion is needed?

The situation is discussed with the agreed contact. Additional support or an appropriate referral may be considered for the case.
