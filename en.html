Your questions

A clear relationship.
From the first conversation.

Let’s discuss your organisation and the right scope of support for your practice.

Will my patients see the Blanche brand?

The offering is designed to be presented under your practice’s identity. Personalisation options and mandatory information on devices and documents are specified during setup.

How do you help attract patients?

We work on your online visibility, content and enquiry journey. Scope, budget and indicators are agreed together. No patient volume is guaranteed.

Can I start if I am new to aligners?

We start by discussing your experience and needs. Training and support are tailored accordingly, with cases suited to your skills.

Who decides on the treatment plan?

We support planning and clinical discussion. The treating practitioner remains responsible for examination, diagnosis, prescription, plan approval and patient follow-up.

What happens if a clinical difficulty arises?

You can request a discussion of the case. Depending on the situation, personalised support may be arranged or referral to a suitable professional considered. The scope is defined case by case.

What are the prices and conditions?

A proposal is prepared based on the agreed personalisation, training, support, production and visibility activities. Services and commitments are formalised before launch.
