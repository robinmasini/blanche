An orthodontist and a local point of contact.

An orthodontist based in Aix-en-Provence, Dr Renaud Desouches sees adults, children and adolescents at his practice. As Blanche ambassador, he shares his expertise with clinicians who want to introduce aligners into their clinics.

With his team, he connects your everyday practice with specialist orthodontic support. You retain your identity and the relationship with your patients.

The practice team welcomes patients and helps organise discussions, appointments and referrals. A local point of support for your practice and patients.
