Support for complex cases

A clinical question deserves a conversation.

A case may raise a question during selection, planning or follow-up. Blanche offers a framework to discuss the situation and identify suitable support, giving you a point of contact when a decision needs further reflection.

Clinical support helps you reflect on the available information. It does not replace examination of the patient. You retain diagnosis, prescribing, treatment decisions and organisation of follow-up.

Scope agreed for each situation.

Contacts, availability, arrangements and any additional costs are defined in your offer or for the case. This is not an emergency service; the treating clinician remains responsible for organising care and responding to the patient’s needs.

Before you start

Can I raise a question during follow-up?

You can raise a question through the agreed channel. Required records and the type of support are then defined for the situation.

Can every case be supported?

Scope is assessed against the case, competence and available resources. Referral to an appropriate professional may be preferable.

Is support included in my offer?

Included services and any additional interventions are specified in the proposal. Unlimited support is not assumed.
