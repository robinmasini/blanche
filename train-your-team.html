Train your team

Shared understanding. A better-prepared team.

An aligner service also depends on how the practice is organised. Blanche helps identify training needs and prepare a programme suited to your experience, with clear roles for each team member.

Understand the whole journey.

Training structured around the stages your practice needs to organise.

01

The foundations

Understand the main stages, the information to gather and the limits of your professional scope. The programme is defined around your experience.

02

Preparing cases

Organise the collection and transfer of necessary records and discussions about the plan. The aim is to establish a shared way of working.

03

Practice organisation

Clarify roles, prepare appointments and coordinate patient information. The treating clinician retains responsibility for the clinical acts and decisions within their remit.

Learn through practical situations.

Discussions may use models, example cases and reference materials. Practical content, instructors and arrangements are specified in your programme, connecting the concepts to everyday practice.

A framework suited to your practice.

Training provides a method and reference points; it does not replace professional qualifications or assessment of the clinician’s competence. Duration, location, materials and any follow-up are defined in the proposal.

Before you start

Can I join as a beginner?

An initial discussion identifies your needs and a suitable programme. Cases discussed must remain within your competence.

Can my team receive training?

We define participants and content according to their functions, respecting each person’s role.

Does training lead to certification?

No certification or funding is assumed. Any such arrangements are explicitly stated in the proposed programme.
